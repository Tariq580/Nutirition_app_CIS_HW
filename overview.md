5 features of this app: 

1- Nutrition tracking
* It will track the food I eat daily: breakfast, lunch, and dinner, and then calculate the nutritional facts such as protein, calories, fat, etc. 
2- Diet planning for weight gain and loss
* It will create food plans for different purposes such as  weight gain, loss, and lean bulk. 
3- Reminders to eat based on daily and weekly goals
* It will have reminders to eat my meals on time, like 4 or 5 times a day at specific times. 
4- Recommendations for food based on daily and weekly goals
* It will recommend what kind of food is good for my daily and weekly goals, like calorie intake. 
5- Workout plans
* It will have workout plans, like 3x a week, with at least one or two exercises for each body part.
